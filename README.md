# PokeMMO Catchrate Calculator

A free, unofficial, fan-made PokeMMO catching reference and calculator.

## What it does

- Find Pokémon by name or Pokédex number.
- Compare estimated capture chances at full HP or 1 HP, with no status, paralysis, or sleep.
- View conditional Poké Ball multipliers and their requirements in the Ball Guide.
- Find NPC shop prices, regions, and Apricorn crafting information under Prices & Locations.
- Works offline after downloading `index.html`; no sign-in or personal information required.

## How to use

Open the published GitHub Pages link or download and open `index.html` in your browser.
Search for a Pokémon, then choose HP and status. The calculator shows potential ball bonuses with their activation conditions. Some encounters and bonuses require extra conditions that aren't checked automatically.
